You're posting videos, but the views aren't coming. It's one of the most frustrating places to be, and usually it comes down to a few fixable issues.

## Your thumbnails and titles are doing too little

On YouTube, the thumbnail and title are the ad for your video. If they don't earn the click, nothing else matters, not your editing, not your content. Invest more time here than feels reasonable. A great video with a weak thumbnail underperforms a good video with a great one.

## You're not hooking viewers in the first 15 seconds

YouTube rewards videos that keep people watching. The opening seconds decide whether viewers stay or bounce. Cut the long intros. Get to the value fast and tell people what they'll get by staying.

## You're inconsistent

The algorithm favors channels it can predict. A steady schedule, even just once a week, signals reliability and helps YouTube learn who to show your videos to. Sporadic uploads reset that momentum.

## You're ignoring the data

Your analytics tell you exactly where viewers drop off and which videos bring new subscribers. Most creators never look. Study the retention graph, double down on what works, and quietly retire what doesn't.

## The fix

Consistency, strong packaging, and a fast hook will outperform fancy editing almost every time. Nail those fundamentals first, then refine.
