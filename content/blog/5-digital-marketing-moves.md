Growing a business online can feel overwhelming, there are a hundred tactics and only so many hours in the day. The good news? A handful of moves deliver most of the results. Here are five worth prioritizing.

## 1. Get your Google Business Profile right

For local and service businesses, your Google Business Profile is often the first thing people see. Fill out every field, add real photos, and ask happy customers for reviews. It's free and it directly influences whether people choose you over a competitor.

## 2. Fix your website's first impression

Visitors decide in seconds. Make sure your site loads fast, works on mobile, and states clearly what you do and how to contact you. A clean, confident homepage beats a cluttered one every time.

## 3. Show up consistently on one channel

You don't need to be everywhere. Pick the one platform where your customers actually spend time and post consistently. Consistency beats intensity, three good posts a week for a year outperforms a burst of daily posts that fizzles out.

## 4. Start collecting emails

Social platforms change their rules constantly. An email list is something you own. Offer something useful, a guide, a discount, a checklist, in exchange for an email, and you build a direct line to your audience.

## 5. Track what matters

You can't improve what you don't measure. Set up basic analytics and watch a few key numbers: where visitors come from, what they do, and how many turn into leads. Small, informed tweaks compound over time.
