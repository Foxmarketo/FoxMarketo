One of the most common questions we hear: should I invest in SEO or run paid ads? The honest answer is that they solve different problems, and the right choice depends on where your business is today.

## Paid ads: speed

Paid ads turn on like a tap. Launch a campaign and you can have traffic within hours. That makes them ideal when you need results now, a launch, a promotion, or testing whether an offer resonates. The catch: the moment you stop paying, the traffic stops.

## SEO: compounding

SEO is slower to start but compounds. A page that ranks well keeps bringing in visitors month after month without paying per click. It's an asset that grows in value. The catch: it takes time and consistent effort before it pays off.

## A simple way to decide

If you need leads this month, start with paid ads. If you're building for the long term and can be patient, invest in SEO. Most growing businesses eventually do both, ads for immediate traffic, SEO for durable, lower-cost growth underneath.

## The smartest move

Use paid ads to learn fast, which messages convert, which keywords buyers actually use, then feed those lessons into your SEO and content. The two channels make each other stronger.
