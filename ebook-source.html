The Playbook

The First Three Moves

1

Fix your foundation before you spend

Before running a single ad, make sure your website loads fast, works on mobile, and has one clear call to action per page. Paid traffic to a weak site just burns money.

2

Own one channel before adding another

Spreading thin across five platforms beats no one. Pick the single channel where your buyers actually are, dominate it for 90 days, then expand.

3

Turn every ad into a measurable test

Every campaign should answer a question: which headline, which audience, which offer. Track cost per lead, not just clicks, and kill what doesn't convert quickly.

Quick win: Add a single, obvious "Get a Quote" or "Book a Call" button to the top of your homepage today. Most sites bury their main action, and it costs them leads.

The Moves That Compound

4

Show proof, not promises

Reviews, testimonials, and real client results outsell clever copy every time. Put social proof on every key page, especially near your buttons.

5

Repurpose one idea into ten posts

One strong video or article can become reels, quotes, carousels, and an email. Content volume without more work is how small teams stay visible.

6

Follow up automatically

Most leads don't buy on day one. A simple email sequence that nurtures new contacts for two weeks recovers sales you're currently losing to silence.

7

Review the numbers monthly

What gets measured gets improved. A short monthly review of traffic, leads, and cost per lead keeps you spending where it works.
